3. QUICK SIGN-IN GUIDE
================================================================================
Step 1: Open the website at http://localhost:5173
Step 2: Select your role at the top:
        - Click "Patient Portal" (Green highlight) to sign in as a Patient.
        - Click "Doctor Workspace" (Blue/Purple highlight) to sign in as a Doctor.
Step 3: Enter either:
        - Registered Email (e.g. patient1@healthgrid.test or doctor@healthgrid.test)
        - Unique HealthGrid ID (e.g. HG-8904-2026)
        - Patient Full Name (e.g. Sarah Jenkins)
Step 4: Enter password: Password@123
Step 5: Click "Sign In"

TIP: You can also click the quick evaluation chips at the bottom of the card:
     - "[Patient] Sarah Jenkins" -> 1-click fills Patient 1 credentials
     - "[Doctor] Dr. Alexander Wright" -> 1-click fills Doctor credentials

================================================================================
4. CREATING NEW ACCOUNTS (REGISTER)
================================================================================
To create a new account in MongoDB directly through the UI:
1. Click the "Create Account" tab on the authentication card.
2. Select role:
   - For Patient: Full Name, Email, Password, Date of Birth, Gender, Blood Group,
     Allergies (optional), Emergency Contact Name & Phone.
     -> Automatically receives a unique HealthGrid ID (HG-xxxx-2026) in MongoDB.
   - For Doctor: Full Name, Email, Password, Medical Specialization, Hospital,
     and Medical Council License Number (mandatory for validation).
3. Click "Register & Sign In Live".
   -> Creates User & Profile documents in MongoDB and signs in immediately.
